# CAT Pricing

CAT is proprietary software. Commercial and organizational use requires a paid license.

- **Personal:** For individuals using CAT solely for personal projects. It includes free use of all CAT-authored local
  functionality, public documentation, and automated help. It includes no promised human support, response time, or
  feature work.
- **Commercial:** USD $15 per licensed person each month for solo developers, consultants, employees, and small teams
  using CAT for paid or organizational work. It includes paid commercial-use rights, account and billing support, and
  AI-first technical support.
- **Team and Enterprise:** For organizations that need shared administration, security terms, or a support commitment.
  It includes commercial-use rights plus the controls and support listed in the applicable order.

Using CAT for an employer, client, business, product, service, nonprofit, government body, educational institution, or
other organization requires a paid commercial license. The root [license](../LICENSE.md) defines the controlling use
boundary.

## Support boundary

- Personal users receive public documentation and automated help. CAT does not promise a human response to support
  requests, bug reports, or feature requests.
- Commercial users receive account and billing support plus AI-first technical support. The plan does not
  include bespoke prompt consulting, a response-time commitment, or an SLA unless the purchase terms say otherwise.
- Team or Enterprise terms may add named support, security commitments, retention controls, or an SLA.
