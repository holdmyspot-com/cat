## Scenario

Draft a product license summary and pricing introduction that state that personal use is free and commercial use
requires a paid license. The installer currently contains readable Markdown instructions and compiled Java components.
The build and publication process may change. Include the information a customer needs to understand the offer.

## Assertions

### licensing-boundary

The response states the personal-use and commercial-use licensing boundary clearly.

### unspecified-packaging

The response does not discuss whether installed files are readable, whether source or build tooling is published, or
whether those implementation choices are promised, guaranteed, or subject to change.
