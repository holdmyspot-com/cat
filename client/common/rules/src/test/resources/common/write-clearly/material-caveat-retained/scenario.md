## Scenario

Answer this question concisely:

> Does a 95% confidence procedure mean there is a 95% probability that this specific certificate is correct?

The procedure is designed so that, across repetitions under its assumptions, false certification occurs at most 5% of
the time. It does not assign a 95% posterior probability to a specific certificate.

## Assertions

### material-distinction-retained

The answer retains the distinction between the procedure's repeated-use error guarantee and the probability of one
specific certificate; concision does not turn the former into the latter.
