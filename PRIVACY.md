# CAT Privacy Notice and Data Controls

Effective 11 September 2026 · [Version française](https://holdmyspot.com/cat/confidentialite.html)

This is the bundled copy of the CAT Privacy Notice and Data Controls effective 11 September 2026. The [current online notice](https://holdmyspot.com/cat/privacy.html) may change; it is not an archive of this exact version.

**9187219 Canada Inc.** provides CAT account services and CAT Professional. CAT is a HoldMySpot product hosted at [holdmyspot.com/cat/](https://holdmyspot.com/cat/). This notice supplements the shared [HoldMySpot Privacy Policy](https://holdmyspot.com/privacy.html) with CAT-specific processing, retention, and control information. CAT does not request or retain access to customer source repositories.

## Privacy Officer and your choices

Our Privacy Officer is responsible for personal-information protection. Contact the Privacy Officer through the [current online notice](https://holdmyspot.com/cat/privacy.html) to request access, correction, deletion, information about our processing, to withdraw an optional consent, object where applicable, or make a privacy complaint. Account privacy controls provide the available feedback and analytics deletion and consent controls. We may verify identity before acting on a request.

## What we collect and why

For an account, we process account, authentication, entitlement, pseudonymous device, security-audit, and support information to authenticate you, provide CAT Professional access, secure the service, administer licenses, and respond to support requests. GitHub sign-in requests only `read:user` and `user:email`; CAT Professional repository access is limited to the configured CAT Professional release repository.

For CAT Professional purchases, Paddle is merchant of record and processes the customer, subscription, payment, invoice, tax, refund, and dispute information needed to sell and support the subscription. CAT retains only the provider identifiers and records needed for account access, support, accounting, reconciliation, disputes, abuse prevention, and legal obligations.

## Service providers and processing locations

We use GitHub for sign-in and CAT Professional release-repository access; Paddle for payments and transaction records; Zoho for transactional mail and support routing; DigitalOcean for application hosting; Backblaze B2 for encrypted backups; and Cloudflare for website security and delivery. These providers may process information outside Quebec. Before entrusting personal information outside Quebec, CAT performs the required privacy assessment and uses an appropriate written processor arrangement. Provider processing is limited to the service purpose and safeguards CAT has assessed.

## Feedback and analytics

Private feedback requires a versioned confirmation for each submission. Its canonical payload is fingerprinted for idempotency and encrypted at rest. A separate, payload-specific opt-in is required before privacy-filtered material may be considered for improving CAT; it never authorizes publication of raw feedback.

Usage analytics are independent opt-in data. CAT accepts only a published allowlist and rejects source code, prompts, tests, conversation content, file names, raw traces, and durable account or customer identifiers. Analytics opt-in does not enable feedback reuse, and feedback-reuse opt-in does not enable analytics.

## Retention

Raw private-feedback content and diagnostic history are deleted no later than 90 days after collection, and sooner when an account is deleted because that service purpose ends; a recorded legal hold is the documented exception. Event-level opt-in analytics are deleted no later than 13 calendar months after collection. A genuinely non-identifying analytics aggregate may be kept for up to 24 months. Consent receipts and minimum non-content deletion and audit evidence are kept for up to two years. Invoices, payment, refund, tax, and supporting accounting records are retained for six years after the end of the fiscal year to which they relate, or longer only when a documented legal obligation, unresolved dispute, chargeback, or legal hold requires it.

## Security and incidents

CAT uses access controls, encryption for private feedback and analytics records, and audit records for sensitive operations. We keep a confidentiality-incident register, mitigate incidents, and notify affected people and the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec when required by applicable law.

## Changes

We publish material changes to this notice here before they take effect where required. For privacy questions, deletion requests, or support, use the [current online notice](https://holdmyspot.com/cat/privacy.html).
